`Minimap` in `pixelworld/engine` (D-146): a world's roads and landmarks drawn small on a canvas the app places, with the player over it, north up or (`iso`) as the view sees it from above, turning with the view, with the ground on screen outlined and a compass needle pointing north. `toCell` turns a click into a cell; `viewOnGround(view)` gives the ground on screen in cells.
