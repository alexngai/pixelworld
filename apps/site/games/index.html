Games

Games built only on the engine's public API, the same pixelworld package you can install: its world view, entities, roads and traffic, with each game's own vehicles and rules.

Drive and fly

Drive Las Vegas Boulevard to Hoover Dam and the Grand Canyon, take off from a long straight road and fly the canyon, then land on a road and drive on. Press H for the keys.

Fogfront

A small real-time strategy game on San Francisco's Presidio and four other maps: gather glim, raise lodges, train wardens, and fight through the fog of war over the level's own hills, woods and buildings.
