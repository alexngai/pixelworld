Pixelworld models

One asset group's models without a world: generated with the style pack's roles and parameters, meshed at the object voxel scale and baked by the real baker in each rotation, by day and night. Footprints and heights are in voxels (a voxel is a 2×1 px top and 1 px tall at 1×). Saving a generator, a city pack or a style pack reloads the page with the same parameters (they are in the URL).
