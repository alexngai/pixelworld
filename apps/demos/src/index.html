pixelworld multiplayer demos

The server runs each game; your page predicts its own vehicle and draws the others a little behind. Alone, you get bots.

  • Dogfight: planes over the Grand Canyon.
  • Race: three laps round the Las Vegas Strip.
  • Fogfront: real-time strategy on San Francisco's Presidio (play the bot).
  • Hold the Block: turn-based tactics on real cities' streets, alone or with other squads defending one city.
