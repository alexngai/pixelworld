# pixelworld

## 0.4.0

### Minor Changes

- eb60b63: API simplification and package boundary cleanup:
  
  - **Root entry point:** `pixelworld` now exports the common API (`createWorldView`, `openWorld`, `cameraController`, `roverController`, `fogLayer`, `steamLayer`, `veilLayer`, `trafficLayer`, and core types) alongside subpath exports.
  - **Dedicated `pixelworld/boards` and `pixelworld/maps` subpaths:** Grid snapshots/pathing/LOS/viewshed (`readCells`, `Boards`, `cutBoard`, …) moved to `pixelworld/boards`, and 2D canvas map widgets (`Minimap`, `TopDownMap`, `viewOnGround`) moved to `pixelworld/maps`.
  - **Auto-filtering `plugins`:** Passing `PluginDescriptor[]` to `createWorldView({ plugins })` now automatically selects only the plugins declared in the world's style pack.
  - **Standalone `cameraController()`:** Free-camera pan/rotate/zoom controller extracted from `roverController()` without spawning an invisible rover entity.
  - **Type-safe queries:** Split headless `WorldQuery` (returned by `openWorld()`) from screen-aware `ViewQuery extends WorldQuery` (`pick`, `pickObject` on `WorldView`).
  - **Grouped `WorldViewOptions` and slimmer `WorldView`:** Grouped advanced options into `render?: { scale, objects, scatter, heroes }` and `baking?: { cache, startTiles, chunkBudgetMB, lookAhead, groundFirst, idleBake, workers, workerUrl, recordBakes }`; consolidated building cutaways and lone ghosts onto `view.cutaway` (`enabled`, `set`, `setGhosts`, `clear`) and hero/benchmark helpers onto `view.tools` (`bakeObject`, `tileBakes`, `timeFrames`).
  - **Projection & veil improvements:** `createWorldView({ projection })` accepts `"iso" | "steep" | Projection`; `camera.screenDirToGrid(dx, dy, rotation?)` and `rayRise(projection)` avoid mutating camera state; `View` now supplies `rotation`, `projection`, `renderScale`, and `night` to every `ScreenPass` (fixing `VeilLayer` under `STEEP_PROJECTION`); `VeilLayer.below` is now mutable and accepted by `veil.set(rect, values, { below })`.
- d431055: Engine: `veilLayer({ below })` veils only what lies below a height (steps), fading in over a step (D-198): a game can dim the street and the towers' faces under a rooftop board and leave its roofs clear.

## 0.3.0

### Minor Changes

- 6fd56b2: Engine: `view.cutDown(Map<object, height>)` draws placed objects cut down to a height, as a cutaway (D-159): what's above isn't drawn and the inside of their far walls shows dark, as solid; only the drawing changes, and the tiles they reach bake again without meshing again; cuts can be given per rotation of the view (`cutDown(cuts, rotation)`), so the tiles of the other rotations, baked while idle, keep theirs. `BakeOptions.cut` in the bake.
- db8e3b8: Engine: `cutDown`'s cuts are baked into a cutaway variant of each tile they reach, kept beside the plain tile, and `view.cutaway` (default true) says which shows, so turning the cutaway on and off is immediate (D-167). Runtime: `TileRequest.cutaway`.
  
  `cutDown(cuts, rotation, { always: true })` keeps a second set of cuts, made in the plain tiles as well, whether the cutaway shows or not (D-168).
- ad4621c: Engine: an entity's `outline` (`[r, g, b]`, or null) draws a coloured outline round its sprite, a few pixels wide at any zoom, over everything, a building in front of it too (D-170).
- 7d72957: Engine: `view.setLoneGhosts([{ object, alpha }])` draws placed objects on their own over the finished frame, each at its opacity, far to near, where it stands in front of what's drawn (D-162): with `cutDown`, the buildings cut away shown faintly as themselves. Each is baked alone (`BakeService.bakeLone`) once a rotation.
- 6883769: Engine: up to twelve entities see through what stands in front of them at once (`reveal`, D-079), each clearing its own ellipse, the first twelve drawn (was the first one only); at `density: 1` the cut is clean, without a dithered edge (D-154).
- 94553bd: Engine: terrain edits and boards at any resolution (D-147, D-148). `edits.cells([{ x, y, surface?, water?, height? }])` edits terrain cells as `remove` and `restore` edit objects: the queries answer with them at once, a server's `openWorld` too, and a view bakes the tiles they reach again, caching each under its edits. `WorldEdit` is now a union of the object ops and `{ op: "cells", cells }`. `query.object(index)` gives a placed object's data. `cutBoard` and `Boards` cut `Cells` from a world at any cell size and keep several to its edits; `followEdits` takes the queries to read cells again under terrain edits.
- c6e4eb7: Engine: `view.bakeTopDown(x0, y0, x1, y1, { px?, tilt?, shadows? })` bakes a rectangle of the world from above, north up, once (D-155): its terrain, roads and placed objects (but those the edits removed), shaded and outlined as the tiles are, as RGBA in the day palette and as the baked indices. `paletteImage(image, palette)` colours any baked image. The bake's projection is now a parameter (`BakeOptions.projection`, `ISO_PROJECTION`, `topDownProjection`).
- 9454a31: Engine: `view.bakeSpriteTopDown(boxes, { px, sun })` bakes an entity model from above (D-164); `bakeTopDown` and the bake take a light direction of their own (`sun`, `BakeOptions.sun`), for short shadows from above.
- 17ef100: Engine: `TopDownMap` bakes the whole world from above in square tiles on demand (`tiles(x0, y0, x1, y1)`, `onChange`, `forget(rect)`), nearest first and a couple at a time, for maps that pan over a world (D-158); `bakeTopDown`'s `cache` option keeps its images with the view's tiles.
- 57a7a12: Engine: `createWorldView({ projection })` lays the world out with another projection (D-195), fixed for the view's life: `ISO_PROJECTION` (2:1, the default) or `STEEP_PROJECTION`, a higher camera with 4:3 tiles. `camera.projection` and `FrameInfo.projection` give it. Tiles, sprites, picking, the see-through around a player, fog, steam and the minimap's outline of the view follow it; its tiles are cached apart from the 2:1 view's; painted-over hero landmarks are resampled from their 2:1 paint (`applyHeroPaintReprojected`). `fogLayer({ followProjection })` thins the fog for a steeper camera. Grid: `Projection` and its presets, `project`, `projectView` and `unprojectAtHeight` take one (2:1 by default), `unprojectView`, `projectionInverse`, `groundInverse`, `sumUV` and `tileAspect`.

## 0.2.0

### Minor Changes

- 4d94895: Hot pools and steam (D-138):
  - **`hotPool`**, a built-in generator, draws a pool on its outline in rings of colour by distance from its edge: mats, a yellow rim, green shallows, turquoise water and a deep blue centre.
  - **`steamLayer()`** draws plumes over the placed objects a style pack's new `atmosphere.steam` names. Each plume rises, widens, leans with the wind and breaks into puffs, dithered into the frame as fog is and kept behind what stands in front of it.
  - **The style pack schema** gains `atmosphere.steam`.
- e1146dd: `Minimap` in `pixelworld/engine` (D-146): a world's roads and landmarks drawn small on a canvas the app places, with the player over it, north up or (`iso`) as the view sees it from above, turning with the view, with the ground on screen outlined and a compass needle pointing north. `toCell` turns a click into a cell; `viewOnGround(view)` gives the ground on screen in cells.

### Patch Changes

- 25eb46a: A suspension bridge comes ashore on its approaches: where a world package gives a bridge approaches (`ap`, the line's run past each anchorage over land, and `az`, the ground where each lands), the deck comes down them on piers from its anchorage to the ground, with railings and a girder along its edges, so it meets the road it carries. The suspension spans stay level over the water. Without approaches it is drawn as before.
- 4b5927d: Core knows Hong Kong's `marketStalls`, `chineseTemple` and `estateTower` (from `@pixelworlds/city-hongkong`), so a style that names one without its plugin loaded says which plugin to load.
- bfc3d59: Core knows Hong Kong's `doubleDeckTram`, `doubleDeckBus` and `boat` (from `@pixelworlds/city-hongkong`), so a style that names one without its plugin loaded says which plugin to load.
- 176f50a: Core's `obeliskTower` and `pyramidTower` take an optional `lit` role and `litFraction`: windows lit by night as a building's are. Without `lit` they draw exactly as before.
- 256a058: A world package may let steeper micro-cells slope (`micro.maxSlope` in its manifest, micro-steps, 3 or more): micro-cells up to that steep are drawn as planes through their corners rather than flat terraces, so sheer walls read as faces. `microCellCorners` and `emptyMicro` take it; without it micro-terrain is drawn as before.
- 28cf99b: Two landmark kit shapes for natural scenery: `stoneArch`, a slab of rock with an opening under its span (a free-standing arch, a window in a fin, a turret beside it, two spans sharing a leg, or turned along the diagonal), and `balancedRock`, a boulder on the neck of a flared pedestal.
- 9095a03: The tile cache key's plugins part hashes each plugin module by its path, not its full URL: tiles baked for one host (start tiles prebaked on a build server) now match the same build served from another. Tiles already cached under a plugin are baked again once.
- 7734fce: A world file without a content type is taken for an HTML page only when it starts with an opening tag (`<!doctype`, `<html`, `<head`, `<body`, `<!--`), not any `<`: a `.gz` a server sends with `Content-Encoding: gzip` (Vite's dev server) arrives decompressed, and binary data such as a geo grid may start with that byte.

## 0.1.0

### Minor Changes

- a1af2a6: For games on a level: `readCells(query, rect)` reads a rectangle of the world's cells once (ground heights, water, what walls each cell and how high it stands, trees, surfaces, road classes), with A* paths (`findPath`, costs and the steepest step a game's own), straight lines walked (`lineClear`), lines of sight over the ground, buildings and canopies (`lineOfSight`), what an eye sees around it (`viewshed`), and edits applied (`setRemoved`, `followEdits`). `fogSampler(world, query)` gives the style's fog as a number at a place, height and time, as the fog layer draws it, on a server too. `veilLayer({ ramp })` darkens cells by a field the game sets (a fog of war), drawn like the fog pixel by pixel; fog passes now chain, one per layer, and a fog field may cover part of a world (`x0`, `y0`).
- 1a64af7: Entities can see through what stands in front of them (`reveal: { margin?, density? }`): buildings and landmarks in front of the entity are dithered away within a margin of its sprite, over tiles baked a second time without them. Tall objects keep their tiles in view as far up as they reach, and the depth range holds their tops, so tall towers no longer go missing or sort wrongly at the view's edge.
- 3cb79a2: An entity's silhouette can be drawn in a ramp of its own (`silhouetteRamp`, a ramp id from `world.palette.rampIds`, at its lightest shade) instead of the style's one silhouette colour, so units behind buildings or in woods can still be told apart by side or player.
- bd7ff4c: The first npm release: `pixelworld/engine`, `pixelworld/traffic`, `pixelworld/format`, `pixelworld/grid`, `pixelworld/style` and `pixelworld/generators`, with types and source maps, and the bake worker beside the engine (`workerUrl` loads it from elsewhere).
- 74015f3: Level switches no longer hang when a layer moves to a new view (bakes settle on destroy; layers and controllers belong to one view at a time), and the view gains game time (`paused`, `timeScale`, `started`, `time`: `dt` is 0 until the first complete frame), `camera.project` (world → canvas CSS pixels), baking ahead (`prefetch`, `isDrawn`, `lookAhead`), controller state in `serialize()`, and landmark plans in `objectsAt`. `FrameEvent.dt` is now game time; `realDt` is the wall clock.
- d231145: Voxel models can carry one-pixel lines (`VoxelModel.lines`, `model.line(a, b, material)`, in voxel coordinates) beside their voxels. The bake draws a unique object's lines as GL lines a native pixel wide at any angle, in their ramp's darkest shade, casting no shadow (`ObjectMeshes.lines`, a mesh with `primitive: "lines"`): something as thin as a wire stays thin.
- f547bbc: `openWorld({ worldUrl, style })` in `pixelworld/engine`: a world's queries without a view, canvas or GPU, for a game server or a bot that must agree with the players' views (it runs in Node). `EntitySpec.wait: false` for other players and effects: their sprite bakes no longer hold up `ready()` (D-111).
- 3dcd417: Instanced objects draw their one-pixel lines too (each variant's lines a batch of their own), and `meshLines` places a model's lines as `meshVoxels` places its voxels. The built-in `streetLight` and `trafficSignal` take `lines: 1` (with `reach`): a slender mast whose arm curves out to the lamp, and a pole with a mast arm over the street and a three-lamp head, in one-pixel lines. Off by default: without it they draw as before.
- 1418bcf: Rail corridors (D-097): a world package's chunks may carry the centre lines of rail corridors (optional sections `TSG0`–`TSG5`, `TerrainLayers.railSeg`) with each corridor cell's track count in the `RAIL` layer's bits 8–10, and the micro-terrain bake draws a corridor's tracks side by side along its line, on one ballast bed. Packages without them draw as before.
- 127734a: Roads and paths can be drawn along their own centre lines (D-082): world packages built with `widths.centrelines` carry the stretches of centre line inside each cell (`TerrainLayers.roadSeg`, `pathSeg`), which the micro-terrain bake draws instead of the cells' connections, so streets at any angle run straight. Packages without them draw as before.
- 6b6758a: Runtime world edits (D-124): `view.edits` (and `openWorld(…).edits`) take the level's placed objects out and put them back, by `ObjectHit.index`, as a log of ops a game can send and replay. The queries stop answering for removed objects at once; a view bakes again only the tiles they touch, keeping each old image on screen until its new one is ready, and caches edited tiles under their own key. `query.pickObject(x, y)` answers the placed object drawn under a canvas point.
- e8b53f2: Traffic a game's server runs (D-126): `trafficLayer({ feed })` draws vehicles a feed gives each frame instead of its own simulation, and `SimOptions.routes` keeps a simulation to some routes, with cars leaving at their ends rather than turning back.
- 148dce2: `createWorldView`'s `startTiles` is opt-in: `true` reads `start-tiles.bin` in the world package, or pass the file's URL. A world without one no longer logs a 404.
