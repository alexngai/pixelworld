# @pixelworlds/city-hongkong

## 0.2.0

### Minor Changes

- bfc3d59: Hong Kong's double-deckers and boats (D-133): `doubleDeckTram` (the island's narrow double-deck trams, in the classic green and cream or an advert's colour), `doubleDeckBus`, both reshaping the traffic layer's box by their asset's `length`, `width` and `height` params; and `boat` (sampans and fishing boats, or a junk under red sails).
- 4b5927d: Hong Kong's markets, temples and estates (D-134): `marketStalls` (two rows of stalls back to back down a market street, under tarpaulins and strung bulbs that glow at night), `chineseTemple` (Cantonese temple halls side by side under green glazed tile with a ridge of ceramic figures, a red door in a granite porch) and `estateTower` (public-housing blocks: white walls gridded with windows, one estate colour in stripes, bands or panels, corridor slots, machine rooms and tanks on the roof). `compositeTower` gains `vertical` (a vertical sign up the front) and `rooftop` (a billboard on the roof's front edge).
